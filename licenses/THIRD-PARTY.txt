USB Link library and external-component notices

Original USB Link code: GPL-3.0-only, with LICENSE-WINTUN-EXCEPTION.
Go 1.26.0 and Go packages retain their original BSD/MIT license notices.
Avalonia and the locked desktop libraries retain their original notices.
See libraries.json for exact versions, sources and notice paths.

Apple software and USB drivers, Wintun DLL, Zadig/libwdi executables and the
.NET runtime are external downloads and are not included in this distribution.
Their original terms, fixed checksums, official download URLs and corresponding
source references are recorded in internal/dependencies/catalog.json (source)
or external-dependencies.json (distribution).

Windows system DLLs and drivers are not redistributed. Wintun uses its published
API and unmodified official DLL. The linking exception covers only that DLL.
No exception is granted for Apple or other proprietary components.
